=== Nexora IT ===
Requires at least: 6.4
Requires PHP: 8.0
Version: 1.0.0
License: GPLv2 or later

A standalone classic WordPress theme for an IT company. No paid plugin, external font, or page builder is needed. Artwork is generated with CSS; no third-party image assets are bundled.

INSTALLATION
1. Upload nexora-it.zip under Appearance > Themes > Add New > Upload Theme.
2. Install and activate Nexora IT.
3. Set your company name and tagline under Settings > General.
4. Under Appearance > Customize > IT Company Content, edit hero copy, company description, four services, contact details, and accent color.
5. Set a logo under Appearance > Customize > Site Identity.
6. The company homepage appears automatically. For editable extra homepage content, create a Home page and select it as the static homepage in Settings > Reading. Its block content appears after the process section.
7. Create pages such as About, Services, Careers, and Privacy Policy using the block editor. Create a separate empty Blog page and select it as the Posts page in Settings > Reading.
8. Build menus under Appearance > Menus and assign Primary navigation and Footer navigation. Without a primary menu, homepage section links appear automatically.
9. Add posts and featured images to populate Insights. No fictional clients, testimonials, or performance statistics are provided.

CONTACT FORM
Inquiries are sent to the WordPress Administration Email Address under Settings > General. The displayed contact email is edited separately in the Customizer. Configure authenticated email delivery with your hosting provider or a reputable SMTP plugin, then submit a real test inquiry and confirm receipt before publishing. A successful submission means WordPress accepted the mail; it does not guarantee inbox delivery. No inquiries are stored in the database. Includes nonce validation, a honeypot, input validation, and per-IP rate limiting. For a high-traffic site, use a dedicated form plugin and spam protection.

BEFORE LAUNCH
Replace all sample contact details. Publish a privacy policy appropriate to your business and select it under Settings > Privacy. Use a dark accent shade and check text contrast. Set permalink structure under Settings > Permalinks. Verify forms, mobile navigation, and links on your actual hosting. Use HTTPS and current WordPress/PHP versions. Theme activation does not create pages or change site settings.

CUSTOMIZATION
Homepage content: Customizer. Additional pages and posts: block editor. For code changes, create a child theme to preserve edits across theme updates.
